Proposed Third-Party Risk Management Guidance; Extension of Comment Period
Federal Register | Published November 18, 2026 | Document 2026-23416

ACTION
Proposed interagency guidance and request for comment; extension of comment period.

SUMMARY
On September 15, 2026, the OCC, Board, FDIC, and NCUA (collectively, the agencies) published in the Federal Register a proposed interagency guidance and request for comment on third-party risk management. The proposed guidance would reflect the agencies’ supervisory experience and lessons learned through examining banking organizations’ third-party risk management practices and would discuss the potential benefits that may accrue when a banking organization aligns its third-party risk management practices in relation to the reasonably assessed risk levels specific to each of its third-party relationships, among other things. Public comments were requested by November 16, 2026. The agencies have determined that an extension of the comment period until December 16, 2026, is appropriate.

DATES
The comment period for the notice published on September 15, 2026 (91 FR 58536) is extended. Comments must be received on or before December 16, 2026.
