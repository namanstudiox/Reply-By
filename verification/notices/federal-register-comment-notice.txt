Services for Caregivers.’’ Based on the
requirements in Section 1720G, VA
established the Program of
Comprehensive Assistance for Family
Caregivers (PCAFC). Under the law,
Family Caregivers participating in
PCAFC are eligible to receive training,
support, coaching, peer support
mentoring, connection and referral to
other available VA and community
resources, respite care, certain
beneficiary travel benefits, and
counseling services. In addition to
benefits available to all Family
Caregivers, Primary Family Caregivers
are also eligible to receive health care
coverage, if qualified, certain legal and
financial planning services, and a
monthly stipend.
In order to administer these benefits
to caregivers, it is necessary that VA
receive information about the nature of
benefits being sought and the persons
who apply to receive these benefits.
This information is collected with VA
Form 10–10CG, which is completed by
Veterans and active duty service
members undergoing medical discharge
and their caregivers to apply for PCAFC.
There are minor changes to VA Form
10–10CG to update the address field and
required PRA information. The
estimated annual number of responses
and burden hours has increased based
upon historical and projected program
data.

131,304.

ACTION : Notice. 
SUMMARY : Veterans Health
Administration (VHA), Department of
Veterans Affairs (VA), is announcing an
opportunity for public comment on the
proposed collection of certain
information by the agency. Under the
Paperwork Reduction Act (PRA) of
1995, Federal agencies are required to
publish a notice in the Federal Register 
concerning each proposed collection of
information, including each proposed
extension of a currently approved
collection, and allow 60 days for public
comment in response to the notice. 
DATES : Comments must be received on
or before January 22, 2027. 
ADDRESSES : Comments must be
submitted through www.regulations.gov . 
FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT : 
Program-specific information: 
Rebecca Mimnall, 202–695–9434, 
vhacopra@va.gov . 
VA PRA information: Shunda Willis,
202–461–1569, VAPRA@va.gov . 
SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION : Under the
PRA of 1995, Federal agencies must
obtain approval from the Office of
Management and Budget (OMB) for each
collection of information they conduct
or sponsor. This request for comment is
being made pursuant to Section
3506(c)(2)(A) of the PRA.
With respect to the following
collection of information, VHA invites
comments on: (1) whether the proposed
collection of information is necessary
for the proper performance of VHA’s
functions, including whether the
information will have practical utility;
(2) the accuracy of VHA’s estimate of
the burden of the proposed collection of
information; (3) ways to enhance the
quality, utility, and clarity of the
information to be collected; and (4)
ways to minimize the burden of the
collection of information on
respondents, including through the use
of automated collection techniques or
the use of other forms of information
technology.

Abstract: This information collection
is authorized under the Debt Collection
Improvement Act of 1996 (31 U.S.C.
3701, Pub. L. 104–134, 31001); the
Federal Data Quality Act (also known as
the Information Quality Act) of 2000
(Publ. L. 106–555, 515); and the 21st
Century Integrated Digital Experience
Act of 2019 (Pub. L. 115–336). VA–FSC
created VA Form 10091 in 2015 to
streamline the required data into a
single form, consistent with the above
laws, and since 2024 has offered both
paper and web-based versions of the
form. VA Form 10091 has been revised
and reformatted to improve clarity,
usability, and processing accuracy for
establishing new vendor records and
updating existing vendor records. The
revisions do not change the purpose or
substance of the collection, and there
are no variances in the estimated annual
number of respondents and burden
hours.
